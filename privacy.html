Corgdoku — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Corgdoku is a puzzle game made for personal use. The short version: it does not collect anything about you, and nothing you do in it leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Corgdoku has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, and we have no way to identify you.

What the app stores on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your own device, using your browser's local storage. That includes:

  • which level you have reached
  • your lifetime stats (games played, win streak, best score, fastest and longest wins, corgis found, flawless wins, paws saved)
  • your best score for each board size
  • your chosen colour theme and whether sound is muted
  • your daily puzzle results
  • a puzzle you have started but not finished, so it can be resumed

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent anywhere and we cannot see it. You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Network and third parties

Corgdoku loads its fonts and its confetti animation from its own files rather than from any third-party service, so simply playing the game does not make requests to anyone else. The game is playable offline. There are no third-party SDKs in the app.

Children

Corgdoku is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.
