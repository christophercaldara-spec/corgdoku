Corgdoku — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Corgdoku is a puzzle game made for personal use. The short version: unless you choose to sign in, it collects nothing about you and nothing you do in it leaves your device. Signing in is entirely optional, and the game is fully playable without it.

What we collect

If you do not sign in: nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting. In that state the app makes no requests to anyone at all and we have no way to identify you.

If you choose to sign in with Google, so that your progress is backed up and can be restored on another phone, then from that point on the app stores the following in Google Firebase:

  • your Google account identifier and the display name on that account
  • the level you have reached and your lifetime stats
  • which corgi and paw print you have chosen
  • the pack you belong to, if you join one

We never see or store your Google password — sign-in happens with Google directly, and the app only receives an identifier.

Packs

If you join a pack, the other members of that pack can see your display name, your level, your stats and your chosen corgi. They cannot see anything else, and nobody outside the pack can see any of it. A pack is only joinable by someone who has been given its code. You can leave a pack at any time from the settings screen.

Deleting your data

Signing out stops anything further being sent. To have the stored copy of your progress deleted entirely, email the address at the bottom of this page and it will be removed.

What the app stores on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your own device, using your browser's local storage. That includes:

  • which level you have reached
  • your lifetime stats (games played, win streak, best score, fastest and longest wins, corgis found, flawless wins, paws saved)
  • your best score for each board size
  • your chosen colour theme and whether sound is muted
  • your daily puzzle results
  • a puzzle you have started but not finished, so it can be resumed

While you are signed out this information never leaves your device. You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. If you sign in, a copy of the progress listed above is also kept in Firebase so it can be restored.

Network and third parties

Corgdoku loads its fonts and its confetti animation from its own files rather than from any third-party service, and the game is playable offline. While you are signed out, playing the game makes no requests to anyone else and no third-party code is loaded at all.

Only when you sign in does the app load the Google Firebase SDK and talk to Firebase, which stores the data listed above. Firebase is operated by Google and handles that data under Firebase's privacy terms.

Children

Corgdoku is suitable for all ages. It collects nothing from anyone who does not sign in, and signing in requires a Google account.
